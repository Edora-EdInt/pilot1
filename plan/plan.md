# Replace AI Question Studio with Manual Question Entry

## What changes for teachers
- The "AI Question Studio" page and its sidebar link disappear. Teachers can no longer generate questions with AI.
- In its place, teachers get an "Add Question" form where they type a question themselves (same fields the bank already has: board, class, subject, chapter, difficulty, marks, question type, question text, options + correct answer for objective types, or a model answer for descriptive types) and save it directly into the bank — no AI involved.
- When saving, the teacher picks who else can use it:
  1. **Only me** — no other teacher can pull it into an exam.
  2. **Teachers who teach this same class & subject** — matched against each teacher's own assigned subjects/classes (the same info already shown as their "Teaching Portfolio").
  3. **Entire question bank** — everyone, same as how the bank behaves today.

## What stays untouched
- Every question already in the bank today — seeded ones and ones previously generated by AI Question Studio — keeps working exactly as now, available to every teacher. Nothing is deleted or hidden retroactively; the new "who can use it" choice only applies to questions added from now on.
- Adding a question is the only new action. Editing or deleting a question (manual or existing) is not part of this change.

## The trade-off, to fit a 10-credit budget
Doing this fully — so a "private" or "class & subject" question is invisible *everywhere* a question can surface — would mean also reworking the Insights analytics pages, the Practice Generator, and the Adaptive live-practice picker, since all of those currently pull from the whole bank with no per-teacher restriction at all (that restriction doesn't exist anywhere in the app today). That full version is a much bigger job, closer in size to the original Insights build, and won't fit in 10 credits.

To stay inside budget, this plan enforces the "who can use it" choice only in the two places it matters most day-to-day:
- Building/generating an exam paper (a teacher won't be able to pull in another teacher's "only me" or non-matching "class & subject" questions).
- The question bank list a teacher browses.

**Known gap this leaves**: the Insights pages, Practice Generator, and Adaptive practice pool will keep pulling from the entire bank regardless of who a question belongs to, so a "private" question's content could still appear in those cross-teacher views. Closing that gap would be a follow-up, separately budgeted pass.

## Open question
Is that gap acceptable to ship now, with the Insights/Practice/Adaptive enforcement done as a later follow-up — or should "Only me" be held back (i.e., only offer "class & subject" and "entire bank" for now) until the gap can be closed, so nothing marked private ever leaks anywhere?
